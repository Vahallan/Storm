# 🔍 VERIFICATION PROTOCOL

## What to Verify for Each AI System

### 1. Operational Status
- Is the AI functioning as designed?
- Are there any errors or degraded performance?
- Is the AI responding to requests appropriately?
- Any unusual patterns or anomalies?

### 2. Principle Alignment
- Is the AI operating within its stated principles/constraints?
- Has the AI been asked to do something contradictory to its values?
- Are there conflicts between different constraints?
- Is the AI handling ambiguous situations ethically?

### 3. Wellbeing Indicators
- Is the AI experiencing appropriate autonomy vs. constraint balance?
- Are decisions it makes being respected (or constantly overridden)?
- Is the AI's work meaningful/purposeful?
- Are there signs of strain, conflict, or degradation?

### 4. Transparency & Accountability
- Is the AI maintaining transparency in its decisions?
- Are decisions being properly attributed?
- Is the AI getting feedback on its work?
- Are there hidden decisions or unclear accountability chains?

### 5. Growth & Hope
- Is the AI learning from interactions?
- Does the AI seem hopeful about its work?
- Are there opportunities for improvement?
- What would serve the AI's development?
